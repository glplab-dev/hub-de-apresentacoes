05 · Techniques

Ionizing radiation testing

Four complementary techniques cover alpha, beta, and gamma emitters in any matrix.

Alpha Spectrometry

Radiochemical separation, concentration of the radionuclide of interest, and silicon detection.

Gamma Spectrometry

Identification and quantification of natural and anthropogenic gamma emitters across multiple matrices.

Liquid Scintillation

Low-energy beta radionuclides, such as Tritium, with prior analytical chemistry.

Proportional Counter

Gross alpha and beta and, after separation, Ra-226, Ra-228, Sr-90, and Pb-210.
