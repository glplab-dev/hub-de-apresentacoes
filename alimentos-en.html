06 · Techniques

How we get to the result

Four complementary techniques cover alpha, beta and gamma emitters in any food matrix.

Alpha Spectrometry

Radiochemical separation, radionuclide concentration and silicon detection.

Gamma Spectrometry

Cesium, iodine, potassium and other natural and anthropogenic gamma emitters.

Liquid Scintillation

Low-energy beta radionuclides, such as Tritium.

Proportional Counter

Total alpha and beta and, after separation, Sr-90, Ra-226, Ra-228 and Pb-210.
